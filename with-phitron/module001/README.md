### Perceptron Output

#### A perceptron has inputs $x_1 = 2$, $x_2 = 3$, weights $w_1 = 0.5$, $w_2 = 1$, and bias $b = 1$. Calculate the weighted sum (before activation).

##### Solution

We have,
$X = \begin{bmatrix} x_1 & x_2 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 3 \end{bmatrix}$
$W = \begin{bmatrix} w_1 & w_2 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.5 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$
$b = 1$
So, wighted sum $= W \cdot X + b = (0.5 \times 2 + 1 \times 3) + 1 = 5$.
